<a style="text-decoration:none; font-size:40px; color:#636363">Hallo!</a> <br><br>

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the <a href="https://ista.ac.at/" style="text-decoration:none">Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA)</a>, working with <a href="https://pub.ista.ac.at/~kchatterjee/" style="text-decoration:none">Krishnendu Chatterjee</a>.

My research work is mainly focused on the formal verification and synthesis of cyber-physical systems. I am especially interested in logic, reactive synthesis, and games on graphs.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the <a href="https://www.mpi-sws.org/" style="text-decoration:none">Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI-SWS)</a>, working in the <a href="https://cossy.mpi-sws.org/" style="text-decoration:none; color:inherit">Control Software Systems group</a> under <a href="https://wp.mpi-sws.org/akschmuck/" style="text-decoration:none">Anne-Kathrin Schmuck</a>. My PhD research focused on utilizing permissive assumptions in logical controller synthesis for cyber-physical systems. You can access my PhD thesis <a href="{{ base_path }}/files/thesis/phd-thesis.pdf" style="text-decoration:none">here</a>.

I completed my Masters in Computer Science and Bachelors in Mathematics and Computer Science at <a href="https://www.cmi.ac.in" style="text-decoration:none">Chennai Mathematical Institute</a>. Prior to that, I grew up in Odisha and did my schooling at <a href="https://shraddha.org/" style="text-decoration:none">Rtapalli Vidyapitha</a>.
